Why sponsorship matters

Grants fund projects. You fund the movement.

  1. I

    Continuity

    Grants end when projects end. Your support keeps the team, the alumni network, and the next big idea alive in between.

  2. II

    Access

    It covers the fees and travel that grants don’t, so money is never the reason a great young leader stays at home.
