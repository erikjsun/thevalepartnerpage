Where the ripples go

Our courses last weeks. What they set in motion keeps moving for years – across borders, back home, and back to us.

43 95% 90%+ 14
  1. 01

    They find us

    43 countries have sent us applicants, from Ireland to Mongolia. Most arrive by word of mouth: almost half of our participants first heard of us from an alum or trainer.

  2. 02

    They teach it forward

    95% of our 2021 cohort went on to teach others what they learned. Their 21 workshops back home reached 369 people in a single year.

  3. 03

    They step up

    90%+ felt more likely to take on a leadership role. A year on, half of their organisations had already seen their work or motivation grow – and none saw a decline.

  4. 04

    They come back

    14 participants have also helped run The VALE. This December, alumni from across Europe meet again in Hamburg for The VALE Forum.
